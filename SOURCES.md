Sources and References

This project uses publicly available cybersecurity frameworks and critical-infrastructure guidance to assess operational resilience, business continuity, and cybersecurity risks.

1. NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0

Organisation: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)

Link: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework

Relevance to this project:

* Governance and cybersecurity risk management
* Identification of critical assets and dependencies
* Protection and detection controls
* Incident response and recovery planning

Link: https://www.cisa.gov/topics/critical-infrastructure-security-and-resilience

Relevance to this project:

* Critical infrastructure protection

3. NIST SP 1339 — Operational Technology (OT) Backup Quick Start Guide

Organisation: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)

* Backup integrity and restoration testing

4. Methodology and Evidence Limitations

This is an independent, illustrative desktop risk assessment. The risk ratings are preliminary and are not measured probabilities or the findings of an audit of a specific organisation.

The analysis focuses on the potential consequences of physical disruption to electricity, telecommunications, data centres, industrial services, and related digital infrastructure.

Any future incident-specific examples should include:

* Original source URL
* Publisher and publication date
* Summary of the reported event
* Clear distinction between independently verified facts, official statements, and unconfirmed claims

No individual strike, attribution, control failure, or legal conclusion should be treated as confirmed without appropriate supporting evidence.

5. Disclaimer

This project is for educational and portfolio purposes. It is not a formal audit, legal opinion, threat-intelligence attribution, or assessment of any named facility.

Frameworks and guidance should be checked against their current official versions before being used for real organisational compliance decisions.
